The FDE Learning Path consists of four levels designed for progressive skill development. Learners are expected to complete each level sequentially before moving to the next

FDE Level 1

Build baseline AI and deployed-engineering fluency.

FDE Level 2

Develop expertise within a specific discipline, platform or technology ecosystem.

FDE Level 3

Apply expertise in realistic customer, solutioning and deployment scenarios.

FDE Level 4

Recognized for demonstrated customer and business impact, not certification completion alone.

FDE Level 1

What This Course Covers and What You Will Achieve

Course Description	Course Outcome
FDE Level 1 is an entry-level course designed to help employees begin their Forward Deployment Engineer (FDE) learning journey. The program focuses on building foundational AI knowledge, core deployed-engineering capabilities, essential certifications, and the mindset required for customer-facing engineering roles.

 

Key topics covered:

Core AI Learning

Common Deployed-Engineering Skills

Foundational Certifications

Communication & Mindset Shift

Upon successful completion of FDE Level 1, learners will have established a strong foundation in AI and deployed-engineering concepts, demonstrated core technical and professional skills, and completed the required foundational learning and certifications.

Expected Outcome:

Gain baseline proficiency in AI and deployed-engineering fundamentals.

Develop essential customer-facing communication and professional mindset skills.

Complete the prescribed foundational learning pathway and certifications.

Demonstrate learning completion by submitting required training evidence and relevant Cloud Service Provider (CSP) certification credentials.

Completion Criteria: 

✅ Training completion evidence submitted
✅ Required CSP certifications achieved and validated

FDE Level 2

What This Course Covers and What You Will Achieve

Course Description	Course Outcome
FDE Level 2 is an advanced-level program designed to help employees build deep expertise in a specific technology, platform, or domain. The program focuses on developing specialized technical capabilities, platform knowledge, industry expertise, and the communication skills required to drive customer outcomes and solution adoption.

 

Key topics covered:

Platform Certifications

Tool Proficiency

Technical Depth

Domain/Functional Know-How

Communication & Narrative Proficiency

Upon successful completion of FDE Level 2, learners will have developed specialized expertise in a chosen technology, platform, or domain, enabling them to contribute effectively to customer solutions and deployment engagements.

Expected Outcome:

Gain advanced proficiency in selected platforms, tools, and technologies.

Develop deeper technical and domain-specific expertise relevant to customer needs.

Earn platform-aligned certifications that validate specialized skills.

Demonstrate the ability to articulate technical solutions and business value through effective communication and storytelling.

Apply acquired knowledge to support solution implementation, adoption, and customer outcomes.

 

Completion Criteria: 

✅ Training completion evidence submitted
✅ Required platform certifications achieved and validated
✅ Demonstrated proficiency in the selected technology, platform, or domain

FDE Level 3

What This Course Covers and What You Will Achieve

Course Description	Course Outcome
FDE Level 3 is a master-level program designed for employees to apply their expertise in real-world customer engagements, solution design, and deployment scenarios. The program focuses on developing advanced technical depth, hands-on delivery experience, and customer-facing capabilities required to lead successful deployments at scale.

 

Key topics covered:

Hands-on Delivery

Pilots and Deployments

Advanced Certifications

Domain/Functional Depth

Customer-Facing Participation

Upon successful completion of FDE Level 3, learners will have demonstrated the ability to apply their expertise in real customer environments, lead solution deployments, and drive successful business outcomes through hands-on execution and customer engagement.

 

Expected Outcome:

Apply advanced technical and domain expertise in real-world customer scenarios.

Lead or actively contribute to pilots, deployments, and production implementations.

Achieve advanced certifications that validate mastery in the selected technology or domain.

Demonstrate hands-on delivery experience and problem-solving capabilities in customer engagements.

Effectively communicate with customers, influence solution adoption, and contribute to business outcomes.

Completion Criteria: 

✅ Training completion evidence submitted
✅ Required advanced certifications achieved and validated
✅ Evidence of hands-on delivery, pilot, deployment, or customer-facing participation provide
